    Checking a signal seller

    • Ask for every trade: open and close time, buy or sell, size and prices. A monthly total or screenshots cannot be checked.
    • Load them as back-test, paper or signal trades: claimed results are usually shown before costs.
    • Count every seller and strategy you looked at under strategies tried. The best of many records looks good by luck alone.
    • Judge nothing on fewer than 200 trades: on a short record, any result can be luck.
    • A good record is not a promise. Check it again on the trades made after you start following.

    How the check works

    Each trade's result is worked out from its prices, less the spread, slippage and financing for every night it was held, or from the costs your file already holds. With a stop on every trade (template files only), results are in R, the amount risked. The chance figure is how often a strategy with no real edge would do at least this well. It comes from re-drawing your trades thousands of times, with a fixed seed so the same file always gives the same answer. A strategy is called proven only with at least 200 trades, a 95% interval above zero, and a chance figure below 0.05 divided by the number of strategies you tried. The method comes from the AI Gold Brain research, where 21 honestly tested trading rules have all failed this bar after costs.
