# Gold tools

Two free pages from the AI Gold Brain research project, published at https://abdullahalhusini5-code.github.io/gold-tools/

- **Gold Reality Check** (`checker/`): load a list of XAUUSD trades and see the result after spread, slippage and
  overnight financing, and whether it could be luck. It runs in the browser; nothing is uploaded.
- **Gold Day Risk** (`risk/`): a daily forecast of how much gold may move in the trading day ahead, never which way,
  beside what really happened. `risk/data/ledger.csv` and `risk/data/record.csv` are only ever added to.

This repository holds the published pages only. They are copied here by the project's own tool; please report a problem
as an issue. Educational research, not investment advice.
